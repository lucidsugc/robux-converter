How do I find my Discord user ID?
  1. Open Discord and tap your profile picture to open User Settings.
  2. Tap Advanced and turn on Developer Mode.
  3. Go back and tap your profile picture again, then tap the ... menu and choose Copy User ID.
  4. Paste it in the box above. It is a long number, 17 to 20 digits.
